Gang Liu, G. Li, R.-C. Yang, W. Xiong†, J. Li†

Highlight: We show that magnon squeezing is strongly enhanced near the quantum critical point, determined by the competition between conditional displacement and dissipation. This mechanism can amplify the metrological gain of critical points.

A²-robust superradiant phase transition in hybrid qubit-cavity optomechanics

Gang Liu, W. Xiong†

Highlight: We show that the superradiant phase transition is A²-robust, i.e. its occurrence does not depend on the specific value of the A² coefficient. This provides a much wider tuning window for observing the transition experimentally.

Gang Liu†, G. Li, H. Tang†, J. Li

Highlight: By exploiting the combined effect of two-magnon driving and dissipation, we propose a protocol for magnonic cat states, which share strong non-Gaussian entanglement with the photonic mode using currently available parameters.

Gang Liu, W. Xiong†, Z.-J. Ying†
